Brewing Ritual

The Kindling pour-over, step by step.

Good coffee is patient. Here's how we brew ours at the roastery, every morning.

01

Grind

Medium-coarse, right before brewing — never in advance.

02

Bloom

Pour just enough water to wet the grounds, wait 30 seconds.

03

Pour

Slow, circular pours over 3 minutes total.

04

Rest

Let it settle 30 seconds before your first sip.
